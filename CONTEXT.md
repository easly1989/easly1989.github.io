# easly1989.github.io

Carlo Ruggiero's project site. The home page is one scene, with one object per project in it and a cat living there.

## The scene

**Room**:
The whole home scene, seen from one isometric camera: two walls, a floor, the desk and the chair.
_Avoid_: bench (for the whole scene), stage, world

**Desk**:
The wooden desk in the room. The mat lies on it, and the old computer stands on it.
_Avoid_: table

**Bench**:
The green cutting mat on the desk, together with the project objects lying on it.
_Avoid_: mat (for the whole thing), board

**Chair**:
The office chair on casters at the desk's front edge.

**Project object**:
The thing that stands for one project, and opens that project's page when picked up.
_Avoid_: card, tile, thing

**Opening move**:
The short animation a project object plays in the room after it is picked up, before its page opens.

## The cat

**Cat**:
The black-and-white cat that lives in the room and does what it likes. It appears on the home page only.

**Prop**:
Anything in the room the cat can do something with: sit on it, hide behind it, scratch it, play with it.

**Knockable**:
A prop with no link and no action for the reader, so the cat may knock it off the desk.
_Avoid_: loose item (when the cat is meant)

**Toy**:
A small prop placed at random on each visit for the cat to play with, which reacts when the reader touches it.

**Held toy**:
A toy the reader picks up, which then follows the mouse until the reader lets it go: the laser pointer and the feather wand.
_Avoid_: cursor toy, pointer

# easly1989.github.io

Carlo Ruggiero's project site. Everyone gets a plain professional home; a reader can choose the room instead, a scene with one object per project in it and a cat living there.

## The two homes

**Professional home**:
The site's home for everyone, on every device: the name, one line, a row per project, About and the contacts, plain and still.
_Avoid_: plain page, pro site, CV page

**Professional page**:
A page in the professional home's style, linkable on its own: a project's own, reachable from its row, or the professional About or Donate.
_Avoid_: detail page, pro page (in prose)

**Room**:
The playful home, which a reader chooses: two walls, a floor, the desk and the chair, seen from one isometric camera, with the bench on the desk. Never on a phone.
_Avoid_: bench (for the whole scene), stage, world, 3D home

**Room choice**:
The reader's choice of the room over the professional home, kept for later visits in that browser until they take it back.
_Avoid_: mode, setting

**Peek**:
Willy peeking over the project list on the professional home, beside "Step into the room": the way into the room.
_Avoid_: easter egg (it is never hidden)

**Tie**:
The tie hung over the back of the chair in the room: picking it up puts it on and brings back the professional home, taking back the room choice.
_Avoid_: exit, switch

**Plain version**:
What the tie and the room's first link lead back to: the professional home.

**Playful pages**:
The project pages in the bench's style, reached from the room's objects.
_Avoid_: old pages, fun version

## The scene

**Desk**:
The wooden desk in the room. The bench lies on it, and the old computer stands on it.
_Avoid_: table

**Bench**:
The green cutting mat on the desk, together with the project objects lying on it.
_Avoid_: mat (for the whole thing), board

**Chair**:
The office chair on casters at the desk's front edge, with the tie over its back.

**Project object**:
The thing that stands for one project in the room, and opens that project's playful page when picked up.
_Avoid_: card, tile, thing

**Opening move**:
The short animation a project object plays in the room after it is picked up, before its page opens.

## The cat

**Cat**:
Willy, the black-and-white cat that lives in the room and does what he likes, and peeks in on the professional home. He is the site's only cat.
_Avoid_: it (for Willy)

**Swat**:
Willy's one paw strike at a pointer that comes close to his face on the professional home, or at the keyboard's arrival on the way into the room.

**Prop**:
Anything in the room Willy can do something with: sleep on it, sit on it, knock it about.

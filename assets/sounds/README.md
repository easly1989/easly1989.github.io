# Willy's mews

`mew-1.mp3`, `mew-2.mp3` and `mew-3.mp3` are the three meows of one recording, "Cat" by
freesound_community (file `freesound_community-cat-98721.mp3`), cut apart. It comes from
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which allows use inside a
larger work, with or without credit.

Each is cut to the meow itself, mono, 64 kbps, louder (peak about -2 dB), faded in and out over
20 and 80 ms, metadata removed. `crt.js` fetches them the first time Willy mews and plays one at
random.

CloudBank

Open CloudBank → View on GitHub Donate ♥

What it is

CloudBank is a free, self-hosted personal finance manager you run yourself — a from-scratch web port of the well-known desktop app HomeBank. You keep your accounts, transactions and budgets in one place, with your data living on a server you control rather than in someone else’s cloud. It is released under the AGPL-3.0 licence and ships as a single Docker container, with no tracking.

Features

Accounts & transactions

Manage multiple accounts and record income and expenses, keeping a clear running picture of where your money goes.

Budgets

Set budgets and follow them over time so you can plan ahead instead of reacting at the end of the month.

Self-hosted & private

Runs in one Docker container on your own server. No third-party accounts, no tracking — your financial data stays with you.

Open source (AGPL-3.0)

The full source is on GitHub. Read it, run it, and contribute — it’s free software you can trust and extend.

Donate

CloudBank is free and open source, and Planora will be free too. If you use them and want to chip in, here is how.

What it is for

Two of the things on this site are free, and I would like to keep them that way rather than moving the useful parts behind a subscription. Donations help with that. They do not buy features or priority.

CloudBankFree and open source, AGPL-3.0 PlanoraFree, hosted at my expense

Where it goes

Time
Most of it. I work on these in evenings and weekends, and donations make the hours easier to set aside for fixing bugs and getting releases out.
Hosting
Planora is centrally hosted — I run the backend, and nobody using it is charged for it.
Keeping things unpaywalled
Bank sync, multi-user and two-factor auth are paid tiers in plenty of apps. In CloudBank they are just part of it, and I would rather keep it that way.

What about Nutreek and Cupid?

Those two are licensed products, and that work pays for the time the free ones take.

So if one of those is what you need, don't donate — get in touch instead.

Nutreek

A self-hosted weekly nutrition planner — plan meals, track macros, and let adaptive TDEE keep your targets honest.

Source code Donate All projects

What it is

Nutreek is a self-hosted web app for managing your weekly nutrition plan. You build a week of meals from ingredients and recipes, and it computes the macros — calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat — per meal, per day and per week, with adherence bars against your targets. Ingredient data comes from a local OpenFoodFacts index (full-text search in under 50 ms, offline), so lookups stay fast and private. On your phone, point the camera at a barcode and the product opens ready to add.

It runs either natively as a single Go binary, with no external dependencies, or as one Docker container — SQLite is embedded, so there is no separate database service to operate. The interface is in Italian, in light and dark, and installs on a phone like an app.

Features

Weekly plans
One day at a time, with a calorie ring and macros per meal, day and week, water and workouts, cheat meals, and one-click copy from the previous week.
Ingredients
Local full-text search over OpenFoodFacts (offline) with product photos, and a reconciliation job that keeps local entries in sync.
Barcode scanner
Scan a pack from any food picker: the product is looked up in your own ingredients first, then in the local OpenFoodFacts copy, and opens ready to add.
On your phone
Installs as an app, with a bottom bar for the everyday sections. Light, dark or follow the system, in four colours.
Recipes and templates
Build recipes from ingredients, import them automatically from a URL (schema.org/Recipe), and save reusable weekly templates you can apply in one click.
Auto plan generator
A greedy planner builds a whole week from meal variants to hit your macro targets — the nutritionist-plan workflow, automated.
Adaptive TDEE
Estimates your real energy needs from your weight and intake trend, and suggests target updates automatically.
Weight, hydration, workouts
Daily weight and hydration tracking with trend charts, plus a workout log with calories burned and a weekly dashboard.
Coach mode
A plan library, client assignment, frozen plan history and a coach dashboard for managing multiple people.
Shopping list and REST API
A shopping list for the week grouped by aisle, with a cart and a two-column print, and a bearer-token REST API (v1) to drive everything.

A closer look

Under the hood

Backend Go 1.27+ (chi, scs, goose, bcrypt, nosurf), hand-written SQL
Database SQLite via modernc.org/sqlite — pure Go, no CGO
Frontend Server-side rendering with html/template + HTMX + Tailwind CSS; installable as a PWA
Deploy Static binary or a distroless Docker image (~20 MB), health-checked
Data ownership Single SQLite file with optional scheduled backups and retention

Open source, self-hosted. Nutreek is released under the AGPL-3.0: run it at home or for your clients, and if you offer a modified version as a service, share your changes. The code, the releases and the container image are on GitHub. If it's useful to you, a donation genuinely helps.

What it is

Nutreek is a self-hosted web app for your weekly nutrition plan. You build a week of meals from ingredients and recipes, and it computes the macros — calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat — per meal, per day and per week, with adherence bars against your targets.

Ingredient data comes from a local OpenFoodFacts index — full-text search in under 50 ms, offline — so lookups stay fast and private. On your phone, point the camera at a barcode and the product opens ready to add.

It runs natively as a single Go binary, or as one Docker container. SQLite is embedded, so there is no separate database to operate. The interface is in English and Italian, in light and dark, and installs on a phone like an app.

Features

  • Weekly plansA calorie ring and macros per meal, day and week, water and workouts, cheat meals, and one-click copy from the previous week.
  • IngredientsLocal full-text search over OpenFoodFacts, offline, with product photos, kept in sync by a reconciliation job.
  • Barcode scannerScan a pack from any food picker: your own ingredients first, then the local OpenFoodFacts copy.
  • On your phoneInstalls as an app, with a bottom bar. Light, dark or system, in four colours.
  • Recipes and templatesRecipes from ingredients, imported from a URL (schema.org/Recipe), and weekly templates applied in one click.

More features

  • Auto plan generatorA greedy planner builds a whole week from meal variants to hit your macro targets.
  • Adaptive TDEEEstimates your real energy needs from your weight and intake trend, and suggests target updates.
  • Weight, hydration, workoutsDaily tracking with trend charts, a workout log with calories burned and a weekly dashboard.
  • Coach modeA plan library, client assignment, frozen plan history and a coach dashboard.
  • Shopping list and REST APIThe week's shopping grouped by aisle, with a cart and a two-column print, and a bearer-token REST API.

Under the hood

BackendGo 1.27+ (chi, scs, goose, bcrypt, nosurf), hand-written SQL
DatabaseSQLite via modernc.org/sqlite — pure Go, no CGO
Frontendhtml/template + HTMX + Tailwind CSS; installable as a PWA
DeployStatic binary or a distroless Docker image (~20 MB), health-checked
Your dataOne SQLite file, with optional scheduled backups and retention

Notesfor next week
Open source, self-hosted. Nutreek is released under the AGPL-3.0: run it at home or for your clients, and if you offer a modified version as a service, share your changes. The code, the releases and the container image are on GitHub.

Updating stays in your hands. Once a day a running copy checks GitHub for a newer release and tells its owner, with the two commands to update; a page left open across an update offers to reload.

Want to look first? The live demo gives you a throwaway account with twelve weeks of made-up meals, as yourself, a coach or a coach's client. No sign-up; it is deleted after two hours without use.

If it's useful to you, a donation genuinely helps.

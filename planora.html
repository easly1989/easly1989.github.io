What it is

Planora is a shared activity planner for groups of friends, family, couples and teams. It exists to eliminate indecision: instead of endlessly asking what to do this weekend, a group continuously builds a shared backlog of ideas. When someone is ready to make one happen, they hit Organize and a guided wizard drives the whole group through the full lifecycle of an outing.

Features

  • Groups and roles

    Create groups with Owner, Admin and Member roles, invite links and codes, transfer ownership, and leave or remove members.

  • Activity backlog

    Rich activities with category, tags, difficulty, links, address and map, cost and photos — plus global search and filtering.

  • Organize wizard

    Pick participants, propose dates, and collect live Available, Maybe and Not votes with automatic winner and tie handling. A suggestion engine and a Surprise Mode keep the details hidden until the reveal.

  • Events and calendar

    Confirm events, get day-before and two-hours-before reminders, browse a month or week calendar, and export to ICS or Google and Apple calendars.

  • After the event

    Add photos and notes, leave star feedback on whether you'd do it again, and keep a history with completion counts and average ratings — or duplicate an outing to run it again.

  • Dashboard and stats

    Totals, most active member, favourite category, average monthly events, average rating, estimated spend and places visited.

  • Notifications

    In-app notification centre with an unread badge, plus optional email (SMTP) and web push (VAPID), gated by per-user preferences.

  • Works offline

    A local cache of core data lets you read offline, while an outbox queues your changes and syncs them when you reconnect.

Under the hood

BackendGo + Fiber, Clean Architecture, REST API under /api/v1
DatabaseSQLite (pure-Go modernc.org/sqlite), WAL, foreign keys, auto-migrations
FrontendFlutter, Material Design 3, light/dark, responsive — one codebase for Web (PWA), Android and iOS
AuthEmail + password (Argon2id), JWT access + refresh; optional Google / Apple
DeliveryA single multi-arch container (amd64/arm64); the apps point at one backend

Planora

Plan together. Decide less. Do more. — a mobile-first shared activity planner that turns group indecision into events worth remembering.

Coming soon on theApp Store Coming soon onGoogle Play

What it is

Planora is a shared activity planner for groups of friends, family, couples and teams. It exists to eliminate indecision: instead of endlessly asking “what should we do this weekend?”, a group continuously builds a shared backlog of ideas. When someone is ready to make one happen, they hit Organize and a guided wizard drives the whole group through the full lifecycle of an outing.

Idea → Planning → Voting → Event → Feedback → History

Features

Groups & roles

Create groups with Owner / Admin / Member roles, invite links and codes, transfer ownership, and leave or remove members.

Activity backlog

Rich activities with category, tags, difficulty, links, address & map, cost and photos — plus global search and filtering.

Organize wizard

Pick participants, propose dates, and collect live Available / Maybe / Not votes with automatic winner and tie handling. A suggestion engine and a Surprise Mode keep the details hidden until the reveal.

Events & calendar

Confirm events, get day-before and two-hours-before reminders, browse a month/week calendar, and export to ICS or Google/Apple calendars.

After the event

Add photos and notes, leave star feedback (“would you do it again?”), and keep a history with completion counts and average ratings — or duplicate an outing to run it again.

Dashboard & stats

Totals, most active member, favourite category, average monthly events, average rating, estimated spend and places visited.

Notifications

In-app notification centre with an unread badge, plus optional email (SMTP) and web push (VAPID), gated by per-user preferences.

Works offline

A local cache of core data lets you read offline, while an outbox queues your changes and syncs them when you reconnect.

Under the hood

Backend Go + Fiber, Clean Architecture, REST API under /api/v1
Database SQLite (pure-Go modernc.org/sqlite), WAL, foreign keys, auto-migrations
Frontend Flutter, Material Design 3, light/dark, responsive — one codebase for Web (PWA), Android and iOS
Auth Email + password (Argon2id), JWT access + refresh; optional Google / Apple sign-in
Delivery A single multi-arch container (amd64/arm64); the apps point at one hosted backend
Free, and coming soon. Planora is centrally hosted — you just install the app. It is in internal testing now, with the Web, Android and iOS apps heading to the stores next.

Social Contacts Sync

Give every contact a face. A desktop app that fills Google Contacts with your contacts' profile photos from WhatsApp, Telegram, Gravatar and their social profiles, and tidies up duplicates on the way.

Download View on GitHub Donate

These cards are contacts too. Most of them are a letter in a circle.

W

What it is

The address book problem

Most of the people in my phone have a WhatsApp photo, and in Google Contacts they are all coloured circles with a letter. Social Contacts Sync matches each Google contact to its WhatsApp and Telegram accounts by phone number, and to Gravatar by email address, and puts the photo on the contact.

For the people who aren't on any of those, it can use the social profiles saved on a contact, and it helps find them: it suggests Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, Bluesky, Mastodon and GitHub profiles with the same name, and links the ones you confirm.

R

Runs on your computer

Windows, Linux, macOS

The app starts a small local server and opens it in its window, so your contacts only travel between your computer, Google and the sources you connect. In English and Italian, light or dark.

It grew out of WhatsApp Contact Sync by Guy Zylberberg. This fork adds the desktop app, more sources and the clean-up tools, and keeps his licence.

P

Photos from your sources

Feature

WhatsApp and Telegram, matched by phone number, Gravatar, matched by email, and the profile links saved on a contact, in the order you choose. Telegram only reads the contacts you already have there.

T

Three ways to sync

Feature

Fill in only the missing photos, replace them all, or review each contact and pick the photo with the keyboard.

U

Reports and undo

Feature

Every sync keeps a report of what changed, with the photo before and after. Undo one contact or the whole run, and export it as CSV.

F

Find profiles

Feature

Suggests each contact's social profiles from your own Instagram and LinkedIn data exports and a public search on Bluesky, Mastodon and GitHub. Review them one by one or as a list. An experimental scan of your own followers is there too, off by default: it goes against those sites' terms, and the app says so before it starts.

C

Contacts

Feature

Every Google contact in one list: edit every field, add, merge, label and delete them, and upload photos by hand. A kept photo is never replaced, and the contact can still be linked to its profiles.

D

Duplicates

Feature

Finds contacts that share a number, an email or a name, and merges them field by field. Google has no merge, so every contact is backed up first and the merge can be undone.

S

Shared numbers

Feature

A number saved on two different people would put one person's photo on the other. Keep it on the right one, merge them, or mark it as shared so syncs skip it.

+

Country codes

Feature

Numbers saved without +39 or another prefix are listed with the fixed version, and fixed in one go.

B

Bring your own Google project

Before the first sync

Google asks every app that edits contacts for its own credentials. Creating yours is free and takes a few minutes; the app links each page of the Google Cloud Console and checks the result. The builds are not code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn on the first launch; the README says how to get past it.

P

Private by design

Nothing reaches me

There is no Social Contacts Sync service. Your Google sign-in is stored encrypted with your system's keychain, and the app connects only to Google, WhatsApp Web, Telegram, Gravatar (which receives a hash of each email address, not the address), the profile pages saved on your contacts, the public search of Bluesky, Mastodon and GitHub when you run it, and GitHub to check for updates. It can also run as a self-hosted web app, from one Docker container.
